Tattoo Deposit Questions, Answered

The stuff most artists figure out the hard way.

How much should a tattoo deposit be?

Most tattoo artists charge between 20% and 50% of the session price as a deposit. Smaller pieces often work fine with a flat rate (like €50-100), while larger multi-session projects typically use a percentage. The key is covering your time if the client no-shows.

Is a tattoo deposit refundable?

That's up to you. Many artists treat deposits as non-refundable to protect against last-minute cancellations, but allow rescheduling within a reasonable window (48-72 hours). Whatever your policy is, make it clear upfront.

When should I collect the deposit?

Collect deposits as soon as the appointment is confirmed and before you start designing. This protects the time you spend on custom work. Most artists use bank transfer, payment apps, or dedicated booking tools like inkLedger.

Should the deposit go toward the final price?

Yes, almost always. The deposit is deducted from the total session cost. So if a session costs €500 and the deposit was €150, the client pays €350 at the appointment. This calculator shows both amounts.
