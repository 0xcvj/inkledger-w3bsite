How to Delete Your Account

Follow these steps to permanently delete your INKLEDGER account and all associated data.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open INKLEDGER

    Launch the INKLEDGER app on your device.

  2. Go to your Profile

    Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner of the dashboard.

  3. Scroll to the bottom

    Scroll down to find the Delete Account button in red.

  4. Confirm deletion (step 1 of 2)

    You will be asked if you are sure. Tap Continue to proceed.

  5. Final confirmation (step 2 of 2)

    A second confirmation will appear. Tap Delete permanently to complete the process.

Account is deleted immediately upon confirmation

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and account credentials
  • All bookings and booking history
  • Financial transactions and records
  • Blocked dates and availability settings
  • Uploaded images (avatar and designs)
  • Push notification tokens
This action is permanent Once your account is deleted, all data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered. Make sure to export or save any records you need before proceeding.
